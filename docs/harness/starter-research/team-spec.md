# Starter Research Team Spec

## Goal

Produce a concise, source-grounded research brief with explicit uncertainty and verification notes.

## Pattern

Fan-out/Fan-in.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| research lead | Scope the question and synthesize findings | `_workspace/research/01_lead_plan.md` |
| source collector | Gather relevant sources | `_workspace/research/02_sources.md` |
| evidence checker | Validate claims against sources | `_workspace/research/04_verification.md` |
| synthesis reviewer | Review clarity and completeness | final brief review notes |

## Handoffs

- Preserve source URLs and access dates.
- Separate facts from inference.
- Mark unresolved questions.

## Verification

- Check that every material claim has a source or is clearly labeled as inference.
- Check for stale or unstable facts before final delivery.
